About Thorne & Co.

A family-run plumbing business built on honest work and word-of-mouth trust.

Our Story

Thorne & Co. Plumbing Services was founded in 2015 by qualified plumber Marcus Thorne. What began as a one-person operation servicing residential clients in the Randburg area has since grown into a team of four plumbers offering both residential and small-commercial services. We're still small enough to know our regular clients by name, and that's exactly how we like it. Explore our services, see our recent work, or request a quote.

Our Mission

To provide reliable, honest, and high-quality plumbing services that give clients peace of mind.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted local plumbing service provider in the greater Johannesburg area.
